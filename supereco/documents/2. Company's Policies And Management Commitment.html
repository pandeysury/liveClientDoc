2.1                          Mission Statement

The Company's commitment is to provide world-class ship management services that meet or exceed the safety and environmental requirements as well as the customers' expectations and to conduct its operations in a manner that protects the safety, human health & wellbeing, the environment, and the property. The Company's Mission statement is:

To set the standards for safe and environmentally friendly sea transportation of goods with modern, technically advanced ships, crewed and operated by motivated, professional, and well-trained seaborne and shore personnel.

2.2                          Vision Statement

The Company's Vision is to be a leader in the ship management industry by promoting its safety culture, utilizing new technologies consistent with its strategic goals, and achieving maximum efficiency through superior management. The Company's Vision statement is:

To be the leading Company of choice for global sea transportation of oil and chemical cargoes.

2.3                          Core Values

The Company’s Core Values set the framework for the desired behavior of people within the organization and create the Company’s identity. They have to be reflected in action and day-to-day activities from all employees. All Managers must Lead by Example with persistence, vigilance and determination based on them.

Our Core Values:

• RESPONSIBILITY through personal accountability for our actions and results. We accept the consequences of our decisions.

• RELIABILITY by guarantying safe, on time and with quality delivery of goods.

• HONESTY by insisting on truthfulness with each other, with our customers and in our business records. We expect and value open communication and integrity.

• OWNERSHIP & COMMITMENT by doing what we promise and own what we do.

• Continuous pursuit of EXCELLENCE through hard work and continuous self-improvement.

The SEIMS responsibilities extend throughout the organization from the MD downwards.

2.4                          The Company’s Policy Concept

All personnel must be familiar with and implement the Company's Policies as per Appendices I – XIV of this Chapter. Furthermore, the Company adheres to the following:

 

  • Non-discrimination: No Company employee shall discriminate against another employee due to race, color, sex, religion, labor union, or any other reason.
  • Harassment and bullying are not acceptable (see SEIMS Chapter 6.1 and 6.2)
  • Articles for Publication: The Company personnel MUST NOT publish any article concerning the Company or ships activities or personnel without prior written approval from the MD.
  • Confidential Information: DO NOT disclose any confidential information relating to Company or ships activities/operations or personnel unless they receive the Company authorization for disclosure.
  • Efficient ship operations require passing certain information to terminal personnel, harbor, local Authorities, cargo surveyors, inspectors, Class surveyors, etc. Such information must be limited to the information necessary for the specific operation.

 

Policies are posted in prominent positions onboard managed ships and at the Company's premises and reviewed annually and when necessary to ensure that they remain valid.

All activities ashore and onboard must be in line with the Company's Policies and objectives.

All the employees, ashore, and onboard must comply with the Company's Policies which are available to the public and the Company's customers, vendors, and suppliers.

2.5                          Responsibilities

The Top Management provides evidence of commitment to the development and implementation of the SEIMS and continual improvement of its effectiveness by:

  • Establishing Company's Policies
  • Developing a strategic plan to identify:
    • Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats.
    • Aims, objectives and ways to achieve them
  • Evaluating progress against the plan
  • Communicating the importance of meeting the customers’ as well as the statutory and regulatory requirements.

The Company:

  • Maintains the infrastructure necessary for the achievement of the specified or implied requirements of its customers and other interested parties.
  • Determines and manages the work environment, both onboard the ships and in the office, to achieve conformity with the requirements of the provided services.
  • Makes sure that each ship has qualified, certified, and medically fit seafarers, following national and international requirements.
  • Makes sure that new personnel and persons transferred to new assignments are properly familiar with their duties.
  • Makes sure that all Company’s employees receive relevant information on the SEIMS in a working language they understand and can communicate effectively during the performance of their duties.

2.6                          Management Commitment

The Company's Management, to make sure that the Policies and the concept of HSQE excellence are understood, actively promoted through leadership and sound management practices, and applied to all levels in the organization, is committed to the:

  • Development of procedures/safeguards against all identified risks
  • Proper supervision of all work
  • Investigation of all incidents and communication of recommendations through line management
  • Establishment of ship/shore communication links to capture best practices and lessons learned
  • Engagement in active participation of all personnel in the application of the SEIMS, and
  • Positive response to recommendations for changes necessary to improve standards.

The Policy statements contain the high-level but long-term goals and aspirations obtained through the following continuous improvement model:

Plan: Effective strategies require clarity in policies, purpose, processes, roles, and responsibilities.

Act: Consistent plan implementation for effective work execution to achieve objectives.

Significant improvements are achievable through clear communication of the applicable requirements, prioritization, and targeting of the processes for the improvement and precise definition of objectives and the measurements followed by actual work toward each objective.

Once an improvement plan agreement is in place between an individual or team and their department heads, they proceed with the actual improvement work. The Act segment of the cycle further bolsters alignment and provides achievement of tangible results.

Measure: Sustainable improvement and HSQE excellence require mechanisms to check, evaluate, and feedback information on results achieved. This segment of the cycle provides feedback to all appropriate personnel on their alignment, implementation, and improvement efforts.

Improve: The organization compares the identified processes with its technical and operational needs and resources to develop a prioritized plan, which is then reviewed and agreed with the management.

Instances where an action is required but resources are not available are referred up in the management chain. The goal is to achieve optimum long-term efficiencies and improvements rather than on quick fixes.

The Improve segment of the cycle helps get better alignment with the targets and ensures that the improvement plans are reviewed and updated. Targets are defined, and efforts are focused on areas where maximum benefit and improvements are possible. All follow-up plans include an exact assignment of responsibility for all improvement actions.
