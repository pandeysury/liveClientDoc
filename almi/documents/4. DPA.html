4.1     Function & Key Aspects

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

4.1.1          Function of the Designated Person Ashore (DPA)

  • The DPA is responsible for performing the following role within the company:
  • Fulfill the role of Designated Person Ashore (DPA) as per the provisions of ISM Code, Paragraph 4.
  • Monitor the safety and pollution prevention aspects of the vessels’ operations, &
  • Manage the development, implementation, and documentation of the IMS.

4.1.2          Delegation by the Chief Executive Officer

  • The Chief Executive Officer has delegated the following responsibilities and authorities to the DPA:

 

Related to

Delegated Responsibility & Authority

Safe Shipboard Operations

     Ensure the safe operation of each ship by:

•      Monitoring the safety and pollution prevention aspect of each shipboard operation, &

•      Ensuring availability of adequate shore-based support.

     Provide a link between the Company and shipboard personnel.

Managing IMS

     Liaise with external parties on matters concerning the establishment, approval, and regular auditing of the IMS.

     Ensure compliance with the applicable rules, codes, and standards.

      See IMSM Ch. 4.3 – Responsibilities & Accountabilities for detailed responsibilities and accountabilities of the DPA.

4.1.3          Reporting

  • The DPA has direct access to the Chief Executive Officer and reports to him/her directly.

4.1.4          Substituted By/Substitution for

  • Substitution for the DPA is given as per below:

 

Aspect

Company Personnel

Substituted by:

     Alternative DPA, &

     Chief Executive Officer

Substitutes the:

     Alternative DPA and Chief Executive Officer in several areas.

General:

        Have significant experience on the tankers’ operations and vessel’s auditing.

4.3.3          Safety, Health, Quality, Energy Efficiency & Environmental Protection

      The DPA is authorized to implement all requirements of the IMS on board the managed vessels.

  • The key functions of the DPA are as per below.

4.3.3.1        Company’s Vision, Mission & Policies and Managing Operations

 

Related to

Responsibilities & Accountabilities

Company’s Vision, Mission & Values:

     Contribute towards the internal and external promotion of Company’s:

•      Vision,

•      Mission,

•      Values, &

•      Behaviours.

Company’s policies and procedures:

     Develop the Company’s policies and procedures for the correct operation of the vessels.

Social Media Policy:

     Support the implementation of the social media policy.

Representing Company:

     Represent the Company in forums, meetings, etc.

Changes in legislation:

     Monitor and follow up all relevant changes in legislation related to health, safety, quality, energy efficiency and the environment.

Energy Management:

     Ensure:

•      Effective implementation of the EnMS activities, &

•      Delivery of energy performance improvement.

Management Reviews:

     Convene management review meetings and keep relevant records.

     In cooperation with the Chief Executive Officer, ensure all actions resulting from these reviews are:

•      Assigned to the responsible member of staff as soon as possible after the meetings, &

•      Correctly followed up.

Office Library:

     Manage the Office library and distribution of the:

•      Company’s manuals, &

•      Other external controlled documents, rules, regulations, Codes, and publications relevant to the IMS.

4.3.3.2        Management of the IMS

 

Related to

Responsibilities & Accountabilities

Administration:

     Manage the administration of the IMS.

   Ensure its requirements are established, implemented, and maintained ashore and on board

     Keep track of and resolve all IMS related problems:

•      In the office, &

•      On board fleet vessels

     Inform the Chief Executive Officer (as appropriate) if a vessel owner or Dept. head chooses not to fully support or finance correct implementation of IMS

Implementation:

     Use his/her right of access to all records, documents, facilities, and fleet vessels to ensure correct implementation of the IMS

     Ensure all employees engage in implementation of the IMS in their day-to-day activities

     Point out deficiencies in the implementation of the IMS to:

•      Employees, &

•      Relevant department heads and Masters

Monitoring Compliance:

     Monitor the Company’s compliance with the requirements of the IMS.

     Ensure that all relevant forms are sent to the Company:

•      At the required intervals, &

•      Are processed and filed accordingly.

Performance and improvement:

     Report directly to the Chief Executive Officer on the:

•      Performance of the IMS, &

•      Needs for any improvement.

Personnel Familiarization & Understanding:

     Familiarize head office personnel and seafarers with the IMS.

     Verify understanding of the IMS by seafarers & head office personnel.

     Ensure that the necessity of studying the IMS is understood.

     Provide relevant guidance as required.

Reporting non-compliance:

     Encourage ship and office personnel to report issues of non-compliance with the IMS.

     Reporting can be anonymous, if so desired

Distribution of Manuals & Controlled Documents:

     Maintain:

•      A list of the designated holders of manuals, &

•      Original set of the controlled documents.

   Ensure that all obsolete documents are destructed. 

Revisions & Amendments:

     Identify areas of IMS requiring amendment, or revision in co-operation with Dept. Heads & Masters.

     Process requests for IMS amendments.

     Control the amendment of any other controlled document.

Review of IMS:

     Ensure:

•      Conduct of all required IMS reviews, along with associated audits and inspections, &

•      Resulting reports of above are correctly prepared and followed up.

4.3.3.3        Audits, NC’s, Incidents and Near Misses

 

Related to

Responsibilities & Accountabilities

Audit schedule & planning:

     Set an annual schedule for internal audits for the office and fleet vessels.

     Ensure that the plan is followed as closely as possible.

     Organize both shipboard and shore-based internal and external audits of the IMS.

Internal Auditors:

     Assign, instruct, train, and monitor all internal auditors

Audit results:

     Monitor, record and analyse audits’ results.

Reports:

     Review and communicate all reports of:

•      Non-conformities,

•      Incidents,

•      Near misses, &

•      Whistleblowing cases etc.

Corrective and preventive actions:

     Initiate appropriate corrective and preventive actions resulting from:

•      Incident investigations, &

•      Near miss analyses, etc.

     Ensure above actions are:

•      Implemented as soon as is practicable, &

•      Effective, keeping relevant statistics/trends.

Investigations:

     Lead or assign leaders for:

•      Investigations of incidents, non-conformities, complaints, etc.

•      Conducting root cause analysis, &

•      Identify any additional hazards.

4.3.3.4        KPIs and Trends

 

Related to

Responsibilities & Accountabilities

KPI’s:

     Monitor and follow up assigned KPIs.

     Co-ordinate the overall management of Company’s established KPIs.

Marine Injuries:

     Monitor, follow-up, and report marine injuries.

     Identify any trends that may help in safeguarding safety on board.

4.3.3.5        Mentoring, Familiarisation & Training

 

Related to

Responsibilities & Accountabilities

Coaching & Mentoring:

     Conduct coaching, mentoring, and training of departmental personnel.

   Help them to reach their maximum potential.

Motivation:

     Encourage department heads & senior officers to adopt an attitude of:

•      Continuous improvement, &

•      Safe operations.

Training:

     Identify training requirements.

     Implement relevant programmes to educate and train all employees involved in vessels operation & management on various provisions of the IMS

     Make use of modern safety training material and researching courses that can be conducted on board or ashore.

Drills:

     Oversee the detailed planning and successful execution of drills.

   Analyse their results.

Briefing / de-briefing:

     Brief / De-brief the Masters and officers before and after appointment as required.

4.3.4          Other Responsibilities

  • Audit the vessels during attendances.
  • Conduct safety inspections of shipyards or other service providers.
  • Being aware of all relevant problems of managed ships.
