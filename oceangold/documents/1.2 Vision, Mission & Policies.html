2.            MISSION STATEMENT

The company is dedicated to provide services to the TANKER MARKET and the public in general at the highest possible level with respect to SAFETY, ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION, HEALTH, SECURITY and PERFORMANCE standards. 

 

The Company’s philosophy and business strategy is to focus on modern and efficient vessels and to operate them with the most sophisticated management systems available in the market having safe operations as the core of all activities.  The Company considers Safety, Environmental Protection, Health, and Security excellence as the equivalent to OCIMF TMSA Stage 4 compliance.

 

The company has set such compliance to OCIMF TMSA Stage 4 as its prime operational target and has officially set the following long-term aspirations and goals:

  • Zero incidents’ operations
  • Zero spills’ operations or releases to the environment
  • Zero customer complaints
  • Zero port state deficiencies
  • Zero external nonconformities
  • Reduction in permitted emissions
  • Operations at a stage of excellence (OCIMF TMSA stage 4 compliance)

 

The company aims to reach these long-term aspirations and goals through continual improvement.

 

Everyone within the company has a role and a responsibility towards achieving HSSE excellence and this excellence can be achieved only if all individuals within the company share the same Safety Culture and concept of Safe Operations as related to their designated duties and responsibilities.

 

The company’s Management is committed to actively participate in the implementation of the Company’s Management System and to provide the necessary conditions to improve performance in the areas of safety and environmental protection at all levels throughout the company.

 

The company promotes the concepts of safety and environmental excellence and expects all employees from the top management to every seafarer serving on board to participate in the effort to achieve and sustain long-term improvements towards a state of excellence.

 

It is the company’s vision to achieve, establish and sustain the following:

  • To provide our services with integrity and reliability.
  • To care about sound business principles and ethics.
  • To build our future through innovation and new business opportunities.
  • To show respect to the concerns of our people and public.
  • To encourage teamwork and open communication channels.
  • To acquire, develop and share knowledge.
  • To manage our business risk and prepare for unexpected changes.
  • To build trust with our clients and industry partners.

6.            CUSTOMER FEEDBACK

   The Customer Survey Report is conducted on established clients to determine their current opinions so that:

• Their future needs may be anticipated more successfully, &

• Any problematic areas identified to be tackled effectively.

Responsibility: HSQE Manager

  • Send MAL-08 Customer Survey Questionnaire to selected established clients, requesting them to complete them and send it back.

   If no reply is received within a 2 week period, perfrom a telephone survey and record relevant information on the survey questionnaire.

  • Record all customer complaints or other problems identified.
  • Notify DPA & any other managers related to the complaint or the identified problem.
  • Annually develop MAL-08A Customer Survey Report.
  • Carry out an analysis and present same during the Annual Management Review Meeting.

Responsibility: Management Review Committee

  • Review the customer feedback analysis and take actions as necessary to improve satisfaction.

7.            HSSE EXCELLENCE & PERSONNEL UNDERSTANDING

7.1        HSSE Excellence

 

   HSSE excellence is defined as:

“The systematic management of process safety, personal safety and health, security, environment, reliability and efficiency to achieve the best performance.”

 

   Company considers HSSE EXCELLENCE (Health Safety, Security and Environmental excellence) as the equivalent to OCIMF TMSA Stage 4 operational compliance.

  • Company aims to achieve and sustain HSSE excellence by:
  • Committed leadership.
  • Effective processes and continual improvement.

   This includes:

    • Promoting Best practices throughout the company. (See Chapter 9)

    • Recording lessons learnt and communicating this information to the company. (See Chapter 8)

    • Following up on recommendations to ensure that all necessary changes have been  made.

  • Engaging the entire workforce, both on board and ashore.
  • Promoting and measuring personnel understanding.
