1.     PURPOSE & SCOPE

1.1        Purpose

  • To document the policies of the management system that enable the Company to provide consistent services which:
  • Meet:
  • Environmental, Safety, Quality, Security & Occupational Health requirements,
  • Interested parties’ expectations
  • Customer’s requirements, &
  • Continually improve customer satisfaction through effective and efficient application of its processes.
  • To provide necessary instructions & guidance for:
  • Establishing Company Objectives and Key Performance Indicators.
  • Development, distribution, implementation & review of the SMS.
  • Ensuring that the Company Management System is suitable directed in meeting customer requirements.
  • Implementing efficient communication within the Company, & between the Company and third parties.
  • To provide information on the structure of the Company Organization ashore & on board.

1.2        Scope and Description Of The Oceangold Management System

 

Basic:

     Oceangold Tankers Inc. is a Shipping Company operating Oil/ Product, Chemical & LPG Tankers.

HSSEQ Management System:

     The Company supports the need for a comprehensive and effective Safety, Health, Security, Quality & Environmental management system to ensure that:

•      Services offered by the Company meet customer requirements.

•      Internal and external issues affecting the Quality Management System and the services offered is monitored and reviewed.

     That management system is detailed in this chapter and is approved and issued on the authority of the Management.

Industry Legislations & Best Practices:

     The " Oceangold Management System (OMS)” is designed to operate within the requirements of ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, ISO 45001, ISM code, the OCIMF, TMSA and all applicable industry`s legislations.

     In applying the requirements of the ISO standards, any clauses that are deemed inappropriate due to the nature of the Company's business, are identified, and excluded from the scope of this Policy Manual.

     Such exclusions include clause 8.3 ‘Design and Development’ and 8.5.5 ‘Post-delivery Activities’ as the company is not involved in such operations/processes.

Processes:

     The processes necessary for an effective & efficient operation of the Company are:

•      Defined within this management system, &

•      Subject to continual assessment for their suitability and use.

   These include processes for:

• Management activities,

• Resource planning and allocation,

• Product realization,

• Performance measurement, &

• Outsourcing.

Sustained Operational Improvement:

     The Company’s management system adopts a process approach to sustained operational improvement.

     This requires the understanding of the Company’s key processes, their sequences and interactions. 

     The sequence and mapping of these processes is illustrated in Doc 1.2 Appendix 24 ‘Sequence & Mapping of Company processes Policy”.

     The interaction between the different processes is described in terms of references and cross-references within the Policy Statements and/or Procedures.

Monitoring and reviewing

     The Company recognizes the need for monitoring and reviewing the information about the external issues affecting this management system and the services offered to its customers.

   Such issues include:

• International and national legislative and legal requirements for the operation of tanker vessels,

• Requirements of its contractual partners,

• Economic and social environment

• Market’s standards/ operating practices.

     Similarly, the Company monitors the internal issues affecting the quality of its services by setting relevant objectives and targets, reviewing its performance and ensuring that staff competence is adequate
