1.            APPRAISALS OF SEAFARERS

1.1        Objectives

  • The objective of appraisal procedure is to:
  • Allow periodic assessments of performance standards of shipboard personnel.
  • Provide feedback on individual performance.
  • Identify any record any additional training needs, &
  • Ensure the shore Management can effectively review and track an individual’ progress.

1.2        Periodic Appraisals

1.2.1    Officers & Crew

Key Principles

Applicability:

     All deck officers, except Master

     All Engine officers, except Chief Engineer

     All deck and engine crew

     Catering staff

Frequency:

     At the end of Probation period for newly recruited and promoted seafarer

     At the end of each seafarer’s contract

     Before the Master or chief Engineer Sign off, as applicable.

   Unless the last appraisal was done within the past 2 months

     In case of Dismissal

     Company specific request

   Do not make an appraisal before at least 2 months on board, allowing for fair assessment of performance

Appraiser:

For all Deck officers, crew, and catering staff

     Master and Chief Officer

For all Engine Officers and crew

     Chief Engineer and Second Engineer

For Gas Engineer

     Chief Engineer in consultation with Chief Officer

Form in use:

     C-28B Evaluation report for CO and 2nd Eng.

     G-11 Service Report for Ships Personnel’s (Junior Officers)

     G-12 Service Report for Ships Personnel’s (Ratings)

Records:

     FPD maintains records of all appraisal reports.

     Maintain the highest level of commitments to professional secrecy (confidentiality) of reports.

Process & Follow Up

ACTION BY

 

ACTION

 

 

 

 

 

Initiating Appraisal & Filling the Form

 

Master / Chief Engineer

 

     Carry out appraisal as per principles covered above in a professional manner.

     Ensure the appraisal form is completed objectively.

     Ensure that Appraisal is completed in an open forum, allowing:

•      The appraisee to improve their own performance.

•      An opportunity to senior officers to express their satisfaction to the individual’s performance.

     Ensure the appraisal includes the following, but not limited to:

•      Ability to perform duties.

•      Personal Effort and Willingness

•      Leadership

•      Promotion considerations

•      Reliability

•      Training needs

•      Participation in Cargo Operation

•      Determine if low evaluation has been given based on inadequate information or conflict of interests, &

1.3        Additional Appraisal of Senior Officers

Responsibility: Key Shore Personnel

  • During ship visits, assess the senior officers on board as per Sec 1.2.

1.4        Appraisals at end of Probation Period and After Promotion

Responsibility: Master / FPD Supervisor

  • Evaluate the performance of Seafarers at the end of their probation period of 3 months.

     Includes seafarers who are new to the Company and those promoted within the Company.

  • Complete the relevant appraisal report as per Sec 1.2.

   Final approval of employment for newly recruited seafarer is subject to Master’s feedback and Superintendent’s visit, if any.

1.5        Review of Appraisal Reports

Responsibility: Company Management

  • Carry out the review of Senior Officer Appraisal reports:
  • During the management reviews, or
  • Earlier, if considered necessary for any reason.

2.            PROMOTION OF OFFICERS AND RATINGS

2.1        Company’s Practice & Policy

  • It is the Company’s Practice and Policy to:
  • Consider filling vacancies, by promotion of qualified seafarers from within the Company.
  • Provide Officers with:
  • Long Term career prospects & Career development opportunities, &
  • Alternative career opportunities, by undertaking shore assignments in the head office.

   Promotions are linked to the Company’s appraisal system as described in Sec 1

2.2        Individual’s Initiative

  • To facilitate their promotion, the officer must prove that they are capable of properly handling:
  • More than what is required from current assignment.
  • More responsibility than relevant to current position.
  • Duties and responsibilities of next senior position.

Responsibility: Officer looking for promotion

  • Take advantage of every opportunity to increase professional knowledge and skills,
  • Request assistance/guidance from superiors as deemed necessary.

2.3        Promotion Criteria

2.3.1    Progressions Tasks

  • Before promotion to higher rank, the company requires progression tasks to be completed as per below:

 

Completing tasks

Officer looking for promotion

     Complete all tasks in a phased manner.

   At times a task may be required to be completed more than once

     Take Master’s signature on completion of each task.

Periodic Inspection

Master

     Inspect the progression task log at least once every month

Number of Masters

Officer looking for promotion

     Take signature of at least two Master’s along with ship names

Office review

Officer looking for promotion

     Upon completion of the log, present it to the office for review and clearance.

Forms in use

Officer looking for promotion

     C-31 Progression Task for Chief Officer

     C-32 Progression Task for Second Officer

     C-33 Progression Task for Third Officer

     C-34 Progression Task for Second Engineer

     C-35 Progression Task for Third Engineer

     C-36 Progression Task for Fourth Engineer

2.3.2    CES Tests

  • Prior being appointed to the superior rank, the officer must complete the following CES tests:

 

PROMOTION RANK

FUNCTIONS

REQUIREMENT

Master or C/E from C/O or 2/E

     Cargo Handling - Management (Deck Officer Ranks)

     Navigation - Management (Deck Officer Ranks)

     GMDSS - (Deck Officer Ranks)

     Marine Engineering - Management (Engineer Officer Ranks)

     Maintenance and repair – Management (Engineer Officer Ranks)

     Aggregate: >70%

C/O or 2/E from 2/O or 3/E

     Cargo Handling - Management (Deck Officer Ranks)

     Navigation - Management (Deck Officer Ranks)

     GMDSS - (Deck Officer Ranks)

     Marine Engineering - Management (Engineer Officer Ranks)

     Maintenance and repair – Management (Engineer Officer Ranks)

     Aggregate: >70%

2/O or 3/E from 3/O or 4/E

     Cargo Handling - Operational (Deck Officer Ranks)

     Navigation - Operational (Deck Officer Ranks)

     Marine Engineering - Operational (Engineer Officer Ranks)

     Maintenance and repair – Operational (Engineer Officer Ranks)

     Aggregate: >65%

Exception from CES Tests

   An exception to undertaking a CES can only be granted in the case where the candidate must take over a superior rank in the case of an unfortunate incident onboard.

  • In such a case, ensure the candidate undertakes the CES on a later date (whichever is earlier):
  • Within 3 months of taking over the superior rank or
  • Prior joining the next vessel.

2.3.3    Other Requirements

  • For a crewmember to be promoted the following items must be satisfied:

 

POINT

CRITERIA

Licences & Certificates:

Initial Proposal

 

Master / Fleet Personnel Dept.

 

     In liaison with the Department head, propose to the FPD Supervisor the seafarer recommended for promotion.

Review of Proposal



Deciding Suitability & Final approval

 

FPD Supervisor & Dept Heads

     If more than one candidate qualifies the criteria in Sec 2.3, give preference to the senior candidate in Company.

   Approval authority is as per below:

• Deck Officers: FPD Supervisor & Marine Manager

• Engine Officers: FPD Supervisor & Tech Manager

• Master & C/E: Managing Director

 

 

 

 

2.5        Newly Promoted Officers & Crew

2.5.1    Familiarization & Taking over Duties

Responsibility: Master

  • Organize the full familiarization of the newly promoted Officer to his/her new duties.
  • Confirm to the FPD Supervisor
  • Completion of the familiarization procedure and
  • Readiness of the candidate to take over his/her duties.

Responsibility: Crew Manager

  • Advise the Master of the effective date of promotion.

2.5.2    Overlapping Period

NEWLY PROMOTED RANK

OVERLAP PERIOD WITH PREDECESSOR

Master

     At least seven (7) days

   Where possible to include one cargo operation and/or a short voyage

Chief Engineer

     At least seven (7) days

Chief Officer

     At least seven (7) days

   Where possible to include one cargo operation

Second Engineer

     At least three (3) days

2.6        Temporary Promotions in emergency

Responsibility: Master

  • If required in Emergencies, make temporary promotions of Officers and ratings following:
  • Notification, &
  • Written approval of the Company.
