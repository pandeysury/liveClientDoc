1.            MARINE-SYSTEMATIC CAUSE ANALYSIS TECHNIQUE (M-SCAT)

1.1        General

  • The Marine-Systematic Cause Analysis Technique (M-SCAT) is a tool to ensure:
  • Basic causes of the incident/near miss are accurately analysed, &
  • Appropriate controls are identified to prevent reoccurrence

   Key constituents of the M-SCAT chart are given in the sections below.
