1.            VISION STATEMENT

 

OUR VISION

Red Sea Marine Services seeks to be a model ship management company built on a strong foundation, upholding best practices and continuous improvement driven by professionalism, integrity, teamwork, and innovation of our employees.

2.            MISSION STATEMENT

 

OUR MISSION

To provide the shipping industry with safe, reliable, and quality marine transportation services conducted in a manner that is cost effective and has full regard to safety, protection of the environment and aspires to achieve a zero-incident record.

 

The Company sets long-term targets and aspires to excel in ship operation by ensuring:

     No harm to people,

     Zero incidents,

     Zero spills, &

     A reduction in the level of permitted emissions.

3.            SCOPE STATEMENT

 

SCOPE STATEMENT

     The Health, Safety, Security Environment and Quality (HSSEQ) management system of RSMS is binding and applicable to the company and all vessels under full technical management.

     The company’s HSSEQ management system is applicable to the following types of ships:

•      Oil Tankers,

•      Chemical Tankers, &

•      Gas Tankers.

 

     The Company scope includes Technical Management of a fleet of oil, chemical and gas tankers, trading worldwide, & incorporating the safety and pollution prevention requirements of the IMO Resolution A741 (18) - ISM Code.

 

     The company has established HSSEQ management system that is designed to comply with the:

•      Safety Management requirements of the ISM Code,

•      Quality Management requirements of ISO 9001 standard,

•      Occupational Health & Safety ISO 45001 standard, &

•      Environment Management System requirements of ISO 14001 standard.

7.            HSSEQ EXCELLENCE & PERSONNEL UNDERSTANDING

7.1        HSSEQ Excellence

 

   HSSE excellence is defined as:

“The systematic management of process safety, personal safety and health, security, environment, reliability and efficiency to achieve the best performance.”

  • Company aims to achieve and sustain HSSE excellence by:
  • Committed leadership.
  • Effective processes and continual improvement.

   This includes:

    • Promoting Best practices throughout the company. (See Chapter 9)

    • Recording lessons learnt and communicating this information to the company. (See Chapter 8)

    • Following up on recommendations to ensure that all necessary changes have been made.

  • Engaging the entire workforce, both on board and ashore.
  • Promoting and measuring personnel understanding.

7.2        Management Principles Defining HSSEQ Excellence

 

Rules & Regulation:

     Comply with rules and regulations, maintain ISO 9001, 14001 & 45001 Standards (as amended).

Safe Operations:

     Ensure safe shipboard operations that:

•      Promote best practices,

•      Aim for zero incidents, &

•      Protect environment.

Continuous improvement:

     Seek continuous improvement, &

     Provide training to improve skills of personnel.

Mitigate Risks:

     Identify risks and find effective means to mitigate all identified risk.

Targets & Goals:

     Monitor and measure HSSEQ targets, &

     Achieve and exceed set targets and goals.

Customer needs:

     Promote reliability of vessel equipment and performance to satisfy and meet all customer needs.

Emergency, Response:

     Remain in readiness to:

•      Respond to emergencies,

•      Contain damage, &

•      Ensure safety of crew and safeguard property.

Ethical standards:

     Enforce high ethical standards and good code of conduct among all personnel.

Health & Hygiene:

     Maintain high standards of health and hygiene across the fleet.

Responsibility: Technical Superintendents

  • Carry out conference calls with vessels assigned to you as per below:

Frequency:

Team involved:

     Master, Chief Officer, Chief Engineer, Second engineer

Scope:

•      Any action items for both sides.

Record:

     Record in form C-57 Conference Calls with Vessel
