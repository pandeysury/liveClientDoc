1.            AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL FOR HANDLING MEDIA

  • Only following are authorized to handle communications with the Press, Radio, & Television media:
  • Managing Director, or
  • Person designated by him.

     Depending on nature and seriousness of incident, legal advisor and other experts are contacted as required.

2.            EXTERNAL MEDIA CONSULTANTS

 

  INFORMATION

The Company uses professional external media consultants to handle media on its behalf.

Name of Consultants:

     Maritime Technical International Inc. (MTI)

Key Function:

     Assist in statement preparation and general media handling, &

     Offer advice in cases of:

•      Serious incidents,

•      Casualty, or

•      Any event likely to attract unfavorable media attention.

Responsibility: Emergency Response Team

  • Be in contact with MTI and advise them of the incident so that they can prepare an initial press release.

3.            MEDIA HOLDING STATEMENT

3.1        Need for Holding Statement

 

  INFORMATION

     Under normal conditions, MTI will give an initial press release, after the MD’s authorization.

   However, it may be required to issue a holding statement to ensure that a message can go out immediately following an incident that is likely to attract media attention.

  This message will:

• Direct journalists to the Company "Information Centre", established by the media advisors, &

• Ensure that the Company can set the media agenda and present its response in a positive manner.

3.2        Templates for Holding Statements

  • Use following statement templates for significant incidents only, that are likely to get into public domain:

3.2.1    Template 1 – General

[Location] [Day / Month / Year]

 

The Company [Office], managers of the vessel [MT Name], built [date], flying the [Flag State], regret to report that the vessel was involved in an incident at <location / coordinates> at approximately [time] hours local time on [date].

 

The safety and security of all those onboard and affected by the situation remains our absolute priority, as does protection for the marine environment.

 

As the Company responsible for the day-to-day management of the vessel and crew, we are in close contact with all appropriate authorities and are coordinating the necessary response.

 

The Owners and [Charterers] are fully aware of the situation and are providing full support.

 

Contact: [insert office name] Information Centre, telephone [telephone number] or please see regular updates provided on our website

3.2.2    Template 2 – Major Incident Involving Known Fatalities/Serious Casualties

   Use this template in case of a Major Incident where there are known fatalities/serious casualties which have been confirmed & reported in public domain by emergency services or other authority

<Location> <Day / Month / Year>

 

The Company [Office], managers of the vessel [MT Name], built [date], flying the [Flag State], deeply regret the incident which occurred at [location / coordinates] at approximately [time] hours local time on [date].

 

As the Company responsible for the day-to-day management of the vessel and crew, our absolute priority is the safety and well-being of those onboard and those affected by the situation. We are working in close co-operation with the emergency services and everything that can be done to secure the safety of those involved, is being done.

 

We are in touch with the next-of-kin of those onboard and we are keeping them informed of the situation. Our thoughts and sympathy go out to all families and friends at this difficult time. Owners and [Charterers] are fully aware of the situation and are providing full support.

 

Contact: For enquiries from next-of-kin, telephone [telephone number].

For all other enquiries telephone [telephone number] or go to our website.

 

4.            MEDIA HANDLING BY COMPANY’S RECEPTION STAFF

4.1        General

Responsibility: Media/ communication team

  • Keep reception staff appraised of significant issues that may generate media interest.
  • Ensure reception are prepared for and not surprised by several media calls.

Responsibility: Reception staff

  • Contact the Managing Director on all aspects regarding interacting with the Media.

4.2        First Response to Media Caller

Responsibility: Reception Staff

  • Comply with below:

 

SCENARIO

TEMPLATE (RESPONSE)

A reporter contacts via phone:

Quote “I would be pleased to assist you. I’ll connect you to the concerned person” Unquote.

 

 Or

 

Quote “I am aware that an incident has occurred involving our vessel – (give name). I am not the person to give you any details, but we have set up an Information Centre to provide information on this incident. Please let me give you the telephone number. (Give number) Thank you for your call.” Unquote

The caller is persistent or says the Information centre is engaged and he/she cannot get through:

Quote “I am sorry you are having trouble. Let me take your contact details and I will ensure you get a call from our Information Centre in the next few minutes”. Unquote.

   Then pass details to Managing Director or direct to MTI.

5.            SUMMARY OF DO’S AND DON’TS WITH THE MEDIA

  • Once a serious incident has occurred, consider/comply with the following actions regarding media handling:
  1.  

     Give initial media statement within one (1) hour of incident.

   First hours / days of a crisis are important in shaping public perception of the event.

  1.  

     Keep a full media watch, respond to media inquiries, and monitor social media.

  1.  

     Be open and honest with media and third parties, gather all the facts.

!!     Do not lie or mislead the media.

  1.  

     Develop and issue a statements and updates, develop question and answer.

  1.  

     Constantly liaise with incident team and update on social media platform.

  1.  

     In making press release ensure that only one story comes from the Company.

!!     Avoid conflict statements.

  1.  

     Express concern about the incident and its fallout.

!!     Do not be flippant or humorous in communications with media personnel

  1.  

     Be honest and truthful when describing the incident or related issues.

!!     Do not try to conceal facts or say, ‘No comment’.

  1.  

     Be factual in your statements.

!!     Never speculate on the cause of an incident or its likely impact.

!!     Do not leave out important details.

  1.  

     Be clear and specific about what action the Company is taking to deal with the incident.

!!     Do not criticize other agencies or organizations that are involved in the incident or its response.

  1.  

!!     Don’t invent/ speculate on an answer

  1.  

!!     Don’t become antagonistic towards the Questioner
