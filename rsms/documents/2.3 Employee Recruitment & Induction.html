2.2        Seeking CVs/ Applications from Potential Candidates

Responsibility: HR Coordinator

  • Consider following recruitment methods:

Company’s existing shipboard and shore-based employees (Internal Transfer):

     Consider existing seagoing and shore-based employees, who:

•      Are qualified, &

•      Might wish to be forwarded for such positions.

 

   As a policy, for Marine & Technical Superintendents start by reviewing masters & chief engineers who have:

• At least 3 years’ experience as Master/ Chief Engineer,

• Prior experience on type of vessels to be handled,

• Consistently performed well, &

• Long employment history with the Company.

Recommendations by existing employees:

     Seek recommendations from Company employees for any external candidate.

Advertisement and Employment Agencies:

     Publish the advertisement on Company Website, Social Media feed, Job Portals etc.

     In addition, if approved by the MD, contact Employment Agencies.

 

  • Include following in the Job Advertisement:

Job description:

     Highlights from the Company SMS job description.

Responsibilities:

     Most essential tasks to take the responsibility.

Qualifications & Experience:

     Required:

•      Qualifications such as examinations, degrees etc.

•      Experience such as time in position, type of vessels etc.

•      Type of personality, skills in English and IT.

Dead line:

     Dead line for submitting the application and CV.

Contact Information:

     Name of Company contact including phone number & e-mail,

     Full style of the Company

2.3.2    Guidance for Conducting Interviews

   Arrange telephonic, video or personal interview as appropriate.

Responsibility: Designated Interviewer

General

  • Include following in evaluation of the candidate during interview: 
  • Professional knowledge in various aspects of his role.
  • Communication Skills.

   The working language of the Company HSSEQ Management System is English.

    Verify candidate’s proficiency in the same.

  • Willingness for training.
  • Confidence, Attitude and Personality.
  • Multi-cultural experience & Cross-cultural values.
  • Reason for leaving last company. 
  • Discuss following during the interview:
  • Company presentation.
  • Job description in details
  • Candidates application and testimonials
  • Identify training needs to fulfill the job.
  • Record assessment in C-27 Shore Personnel Interview Evaluation Form

Additionally for Round 2 Interview

  • Clarify and follow up on first interview.
  • Explain the Conditions of employment.
  • Seek to elaboration of any other exposed issues where further clarity is needed.

2.4        Induction/ Familiarization

2.4.1    Induction

Announcement

Responsibility: HR Coordinator

  • Circulate an internal (office) announcement for the recruitment of the new employee.

Preparations

Responsibility: HR Coordinator

  • In due time, latest the day before commencement, ensure the workplace is made ready.

 

  INFORMATION

This includes, but is not limited to:

     Furnishing, a copy of the Company SMS, Circulars, and Forms,

     Telephone,

     IT-installation,

     Business cards,

     Entrance swipe card, &

     Insurances,

     Mobile phone, laptop, or similar effects as required by the position.

Responsibility: HR Coordinator/ Department Manager

  • Prepare induction plan and induction checklist.

   The induction plan must be tailor-made for the employee to fit his position in reference to the company training matrix.

On Arrival

Responsibility: HR Coordinator/ Department Manager

  • Receive & welcome the new employee.
  • Give him a guided tour around the office premises and surroundings to familiarize with all safety measures in connection with an emergency in or around the building.

   Including contact details on the local fire department, police, hospital, and other relevant institutions.

  • Discuss the induction plan & agree on how to proceed with the same.
  • Explain the organization structure.
  • Introduce him/ her to:
  • Departmental and other colleagues,
  • Others in related departments like purchase, operations etc.
  • External key persons, company Offices
  • All vessels indicating which vessels that he will be working with in the future.

2.4.2    Familiarization Process

Responsibility: HR Coordinator / Department Manager

  • Ensure, all newly employed Shore Personnel complete their familiarization within 1 month using forms:
  • G-79 Shore Key Personnel Induction Form.
  • G-80 Familiarization Form of Newly Inducted Support Staff.

   The Familiarization procedure is as follows:

 

 

   This includes:

    • Familiarization in cross-functional operations for developing interpersonal skills.

    • Practical on the job familiarization, e.g., accompanied Ship visits along with other superintendents/ managers before being given independent duties.

Responsibility: HR Coordinator

  • Monitor the Familiarization Process of all newly employed Shore Staff.

Responsibility: New Employee

  • Within 1 month of joining, submit the filled & signed familiarization form G-79 or G-80 to HR Coordinator.

Responsibility: HR Coordinator

  • Keep records of this Familiarization.

2.5        Recordkeeping

Responsibility: HR Coordinator

  • File following for the successful candidate in the Staff file:
  • CV & Supporting documents.
  • C-27 Shore Personnel Interview Evaluation Form
  • C-65 Employment Application Form
  • Contract of Employment
  • Induction Plan
  • Familiarization form

   File all future evidence for internal and external training in this file.

3.            PERIODIC MEDICAL TESTS

Responsibility: HR Coordinator 

  • Renew Medical fitness tests and certification for key office personnel undertaking sailing ship visits every 2 years.

   Such a test certification must be equivalent to “Fit for Sea” duties.

   If there are medical test discrepancies, an occupational health professionals or medical experts will be consulted  to understand the implications and whether the candidate is fit for the role.
